# Tideline — Meet New People, Visit New Places, Create New Stories

Tideline is a mobile app that turns solo and small-group travel into a
shared, spontaneous social experience. It's built around a real trip
scenario — day-trippers heading from Singapore to Batam — but the core idea
generalizes to any destination where people travel alone, want company, and
don't know anyone nearby.

> **Promo line:** *Meet new people, create new stories.*

---

## 🌊 Why "Tideline"?

Just as a tide brings different paths into one flow, **Tideline brings
travellers, places, and shared journeys together**.

One connection leads to one journey — **Point A → Point B → Point C** — with
different people joining along the way.

The name represents our vision of creating a continuous flow between
**people, places, and experiences**, turning individual trips into shared
journeys.

---

## The Problem

### When People Travel, They Ideally Want To:

1. **Meet new people**
2. **Visit new places**

And existing apps mostly fulfill only the **second** criteria.

### Our Core Proposition

> **Meet new people, while visiting new places.**

- **Approach anxiety.** People want to meet new people and see new places,
  but there's no natural, low-pressure way to connect with strangers who
  share their interests — especially in a public place, in a foreign
  country, on a tight timeline.

### Batam-Specific Problem

- **Scattered itineraries, mostly SG travellers.** People travelling to
  Batam (and similar short-haul destinations) mostly go it alone, with no
  integrated plan — which also means more solo trips and a higher carbon
  footprint per person.

- **Over-crowding in popular places vs. under-explored hidden gems.**
  Everyone converges on the same handful of popular spots, which get
  crowded, while genuinely good local businesses stay undiscovered.

---

## The Solution

Tideline gives travellers a shared, location-aware feed of nearby events
and places, an icebreaker chat to find people going the same way, and a
lightweight group-forming flow that turns **"who else wants to do this?"**
into an actual trip together — with group discounts from local businesses
as the incentive to say yes.

### How It Works

1. **Sign up & set interests.** New users pick what they're into (nature,
   hiking, cafe hopping, beach clubs, etc.) and create a username. That
   builds their profile — no photos, occupation, or other personal details
   required.

2. **Discover nearby.** The home screen shows nearby events and places on a
   map — restaurants, beach clubs, hidden-gem suggestions personalised to
   where the user currently is. If they're already at a popular spot, the
   app leans into recommending an underrated alternative instead.

3. **Start the conversation.** Tapping an event opens the option to share
   it into a **common nearby chat** (open to everyone physically close by,
   like a local, ephemeral group chat). Someone asks "who wants to go to
   this beach club?" and shares the event link.

4. **See the incentive, join the group.** The event link shows how many
   people are already going and what group size unlocks a discount (e.g.
   "4 people going — a group of 8 gets 10% off"). That visible momentum
   encourages more people to join, creating a network effect among people
   who are physically nearby.

5. **Form a travel group.** Joining opens a dedicated group chat named by
   route — e.g. *Ferry Terminal → Beach Club*, or *Hotel → Café* — where the
   group plans logistics, shares transport/cab costs, and coordinates the
   trip together (greener than everyone travelling separately). If a group
   for that destination already exists, joining the event reuses it instead
   of creating a duplicate.

6. **Repeat, point to point.** Once the group arrives at Point B, the same
   flow starts again for whoever wants to head to Point C — the itinerary
   is built collaboratively, one hop at a time, with different people at
   each leg.

## User Base

- Corporate travellers on a 1–2 day trip with no one to explore with
- Solo travellers
- Small groups looking to meet more people
- Travellers who want to connect with people from their own country in a
  new place

---

## What Makes It Different (USP)

- **An icebreaker for public spaces.** Instead of relying on people to
  approach strangers cold, the app gives them a shared reason (a nearby
  event) and a low-stakes way to say "I'm interested too."

- **Chained, collaborative itineraries.** No single fixed plan — people
  travel together for the leg they're both interested in (A → B), then
  regroup with different people for the next leg (B → C).

- **Privacy by default.** Only username and interests are visible — no
  photos, workplace, or other identifying details.

- **Two-sided incentive.** Travellers get company and group discounts;
  local businesses (beach clubs, cafés, tour operators) get guaranteed
  volume and new leads, which is what makes the discount worth offering.

- **Promotes hidden gems and green travel.** Personalised suggestions steer
  demand away from over-touristed spots toward local/small businesses, and
  shared transport within a group reduces the trip's carbon footprint.

- **Milestones.** Attending multiple events (e.g. 5) unlocks bigger
  rewards, like a discount on the return ferry.

---

## Business Model

- **Local business partnerships** — beach clubs, cafés, restaurants, and
  other tourist-facing businesses offer group discounts in exchange for
  leads and higher footfall.

- **Subscription** — optional paid tier for exclusive features, e.g.
  recommending people nearby with similar interests; otherwise, random
  people are shown in the free tier.

---

## Future Scope

- **Travel history between two people** — tap a profile to see everywhere
  you've travelled together and what you did.

- **Enhanced profile discovery for premium subscribers** — premium users
  could see additional profile information and preferences to help them
  choose compatible people to travel with.
